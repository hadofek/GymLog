# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gymgoers and strength athletes who track their own training. Context: brief sessions after or during a workout — focused, sometimes one-handed on a phone under gym lighting. Job to be done: log sets quickly, view progress at a glance, understand which muscles are being worked.

## Product Purpose

GymLog is a personal workout tracker for strength and conditioning. It exists because most gym apps are bloated or over-social. Success: a user can log a complete workout in under 2 minutes and read their progress without interpreting charts.

## Brand Personality

Kinetic, obsidian, precise. The app feels like a high-performance instrument — dark, minimal, no decoration that doesn't earn its place. Tone: confident and direct. Not motivational-poster energy. Built for people who take training seriously.

## Anti-references

- Bright consumer apps (MyFitnessPal, Strava). Friendly, colorful, social — wrong register entirely.
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetic. If it looks like a CRM or analytics tool, it's failed.
- Anatomy-textbook muscle maps with rainbow region fills and label lines everywhere.
- Anything that reads as "AI generated fitness app" from a stock template.

## Design Principles

1. Dark surfaces, high contrast — designed for low-light gym and locker-room environments.
2. Data over decoration — numbers and charts earn visual weight; chrome does not.
3. Speed over discovery — every path to logging a set is three taps or fewer.
4. Kinetic Obsidian system — dark navy surfaces, amber accent, precise Lexend typography. The system is the brand.
5. Glow as signal — the muscle map uses bioluminescent glow as a data channel, not aesthetics. Unlit muscle = zero sessions. Bright glow = high frequency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Contrast ratios must be legible in bright outdoor light. No reduced-motion handling currently.
