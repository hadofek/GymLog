Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2025  ·  GymLog  ·  Israel

Overview

GymLog is a workout tracking application. It does not require an account, registration, or sign-in. GymLog does not operate a backend server for storing your workout data, measurements, or profile. All information you enter is stored locally on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

GymLog does not collect, transmit, or store on our servers:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, date of birth)
  • Workout or measurement data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Crash reports

There is no GymLog account, no GymLog cloud, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK in the application.

Local Storage

All data you enter in GymLog — workouts, sets, exercises, body measurements, templates, and profile information — is stored in a local database on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device through GymLog.

Location (GPS)

GymLog may request permission to access your device's location when you use the GPS cardio tracking feature. Location data is used solely to record your workout route during a session. Recorded routes are stored locally on your device. GymLog does not upload your GPS data or routes to any server we operate.

If you do not use GPS cardio tracking, location permission is not needed and is not requested.

Camera and Photo Library

GymLog may request permission to access your camera or photo library when you choose to add a profile photo or a workout image. Any photo you select or capture is stored locally on your device. GymLog does not upload photos to any server we operate.

Map Tiles (OpenStreetMap)

When you view a map within GymLog — for example, when reviewing a recorded cardio route — map image tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers. This request is made directly from your device to OpenStreetMap's infrastructure. Technical information such as your IP address may be received by OpenStreetMap as part of this request. GymLog does not receive or store this information. OpenStreetMap's own privacy policy governs how they handle such requests.

Device Permissions

You can review and revoke any permission GymLog has been granted at any time through your device's operating system settings (iOS: Settings › GymLog; Android: Settings › Apps › GymLog › Permissions).
